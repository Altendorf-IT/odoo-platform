# 2026-11-17 — The image keeps the translation catalogs of the Hub add-ons

## Context

`Dockerfile.dockerignore` removes every `i18n/` folder of vendored code and
keeps only the catalogs of `src/custom_addons` and of `organize_urself`
(commit 85e7f55). The Hustlers Hub repository is vendored too
(`src/third_party_addons/_vendor/hustlers_hub`), but its Odoo add-ons
(`odoo-addons/hustlers_hub*`) are first-party. The Steering Workspace
(`hustlers_hub_steering_workspace`) has all its labels in `i18n/de.po`. On
production the workspace showed English to German users. The owner said yes to
this fix on 17 Nov 2026 (call F of the Steering Workspace).

## Decision

Two negation lines after the organize_urself lines keep
`odoo-addons/hustlers_hub*/i18n/` of the Hub repository. Other add-ons of that
repository (`web_svelte_host`) and all other vendor catalogs keep the vendor
rule. `**/*.pot` stays removed: Odoo does not load templates.

Rejected: a general "keep all i18n" rule. It would ship the large OCA and muk
catalogs that are removed on purpose.

## Consequences

- Next FULL build: the image has
  `/opt/extra-addons/third_party_addons/_vendor/hustlers_hub/odoo-addons/hustlers_hub_steering_workspace/i18n/de.po`.
  Check: `docker run --rm --entrypoint sh <image> -c 'ls /opt/extra-addons/third_party_addons/_vendor/hustlers_hub/odoo-addons/*/i18n'`
  lists the `hustlers_hub*` folders only.
- Odoo loads the German terms on the next update of those add-ons
  (`click-odoo-update` updates a module when its files change).
- A new first-party vendored repository needs its own negation lines here.
